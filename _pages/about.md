I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Texas at Dallas, advised by Prof. Feng Chen. My research focuses on rigorous empirical evaluation, uncertainty quantification for large language models, hallucination detection, and AI safety.

I am broadly flexible in research direction and committed to moving projects efficiently from idea development to paper submission.
