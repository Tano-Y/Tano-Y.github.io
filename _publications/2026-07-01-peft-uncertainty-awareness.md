This work studies how parameter-efficient fine-tuning reshapes uncertainty-aware hallucination detection in fact-seeking question answering.

The study finds that PEFT primarily acts as an uncertainty reshaper by shifting incorrect answers away from overconfident regimes, making them more detectable, rather than substantially expanding factual knowledge.

Contribution: I conducted all uncertainty-related experiments and contributed substantially to manuscript writing.
